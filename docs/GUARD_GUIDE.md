# Water Level App — Guide for Station Guards

This phone reads the water level from the sensor and sends it to the office.
**It works by itself.** You only need to keep the phone ready.

---

## Every day — 4 rules

1. **Keep the phone charging.** Leave it plugged in all the time.
2. **Keep WiFi ON.** The phone talks to the sensor over WiFi.
3. **Keep mobile data ON.** The phone sends readings to the office over mobile data.
4. **Keep the phone near the sensor box.**

That is all. You do not need to press anything.

---

## Never do these

- ❌ **Never uninstall (delete) the app.** Readings not yet sent are lost forever.
- ❌ **Never "Force stop" the app** or clear it with a "cleaner" / "booster" app.
- ❌ **Never turn on Airplane mode.**
- ❌ **Never use "Clear data" or "Clear storage"** for this app.
- ❌ **Never turn off WiFi or mobile data** to save battery.

If someone tells you to uninstall the app, **call the office first.**

---

## How to check that everything is OK

Open the app. Look at the box under the big number.

| You see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ **Connected to sensor · all readings sent** | Everything is working | Nothing |
| ✅ **All readings sent to office** | Everything is working | Nothing |
| 🟡 **5 readings waiting to send** | Phone has the readings, sending soon | Nothing. Check mobile data is ON |
| 🟡 **Not connected to the sensor** | Phone is not talking to the sensor | Tap **Connect to sensor** |
| 🟡 **WiFi is off** | WiFi is turned off | Tap **Turn on WiFi** |
| 🔴 **WiFi and mobile data are off** | Phone cannot work | Turn on WiFi and mobile data |
| 🔴 **Phone storage is full** | Phone cannot save readings | Delete photos and videos (see below) |
| 🔴 **Sensor is not answering** | Sensor may have no power | Check the sensor box power. Call office if it continues |

There is also a small line at the top of the phone (notification) that says
**"Water Level app is running"**. This is normal. **Do not swipe it away.**

---

## Coloured boxes at the top of the app

Sometimes a yellow or red box appears with a **Fix** button.

1. Tap **Fix**.
2. The phone opens a settings page.
3. Choose **Allow** / **Turn on** / **No restrictions**.
4. Press the **Back** button to return to the app.

If you cannot do it, call the office.

---

## If phone storage is full

1. Open **Gallery** or **Photos**.
2. Delete old photos and videos (especially WhatsApp videos).
3. Open WhatsApp → **Settings → Storage and data → Manage storage** → delete big files.
4. Open the Water Level app again.

---

## Installing a new version (only when the office tells you)

1. Make sure the app says **"all readings sent"** before you start.
2. The office sends a file named **Umiam-WaterLevel.apk** on WhatsApp.
3. Tap the file → tap **Install**.
4. If the phone asks **"Allow from this source?"** → turn it ON → go back → **Install**.
5. If you see **"Play Protect"** → tap **More details → Install anyway**.
6. Open the app. Tap **Allow** on every question.

### If it says "App not installed"

1. Free up storage (see above). The phone needs about **500 MB** free.
2. Download the file again from WhatsApp (the first download may be broken).
3. Still not working? **Take a photo of the screen and send it to the office.**
   Do not uninstall anything unless the office tells you.

---
